How This Repository Supports Every Learner

1. High Color Contrast

Deep burgundy, gold, and crisp dark slate text ensure strain-free readability on phones, tablets, and desktops.

2. Descriptive Alt Text & Captions

Every visual diagram includes descriptive alternative text, and all video lessons include closed captions and text transcripts.

3. Simple, Keyboard-Friendly Navigation

Clear headings, large tap targets, and visible focus rings make navigating effortless for pupils and teachers.

Accessibility Statement

Commitment to Inclusive Digital Design

  • High Color Contrast: Primary crimson/burgundy headers paired with metallic gold accents and dark text on clean light surfaces meet WCAG AA/AAA contrast ratios, supplemented by an instant High Contrast Mode toggle.
  • Descriptive Alt Text: All icons, school emblems, and instructional diagrams (such as magnetic pole illustrations) include meaningful alternative text for screen readers.
  • Clear, Simple Navigation: Consistent top navigation tabs, skip-to-content links, keyboard focus rings, resizable text controls (A- / A+), and closed-captioned video transcripts make all materials easy to access.
